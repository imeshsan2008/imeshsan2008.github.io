Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect my MySLT account automatically?

Open https://myslt.slt.lk/ and sign in. MySLT Tracker reads slt_accessToken and selectedAcc directly in your browser and displays a top summary banner with a quick Copy Token button.

Why do I see an HTTP 401 or 403 / expired token error?

MySLT Bearer tokens expire periodically. Simply visit myslt.slt.lk while signed in so the extension refreshes your local token automatically, or paste a fresh token inside settings (⚙).

How do I clear saved credentials or test auto-detection?

Click the settings gear (⚙) in the extension header and click Reset Settings. All saved credentials in chrome.storage.local are wiped immediately.

Does this work with SLT ADSL or 4G LTE packages?

Package detection is built to dynamically parse every quota bucket returned by SLT's APIs, though only SLT Fibre has been tested directly so far. ADSL and 4G user reports via WhatsApp or GitHub are very welcome!
