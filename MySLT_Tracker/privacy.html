Privacy Policy for MySLT Tracker

MySLT Tracker (MySLT Direct Background Tracker, Version V1.0.0) is an unofficial, open-source browser extension developed by imeshsan2008. Your privacy is protected by design: we collect zero personal data and run no external servers.

1. Data Accessed & Local-Only Storage

To fetch your own broadband usage and billing summary directly from official SLT APIs, the extension handles only the following session values on your local device:

  • sltToken — Your MySLT Bearer access token read from https://myslt.slt.lk/ session storage or entered manually by you.
  • sltPhone — Your SLT telephone / subscriber number used strictly as the query parameter when requesting your usage and bill.
  • sltAcc — Your SLT account number used strictly to query your account billing details.
  • sltNoticeDismissed / banner dismiss state — Simple UI preference flags stored locally.

All of these values are stored strictly on your computer via Chrome's chrome.storage.local API. Nothing is ever transmitted to the developer, analytics providers, ad networks, or any third-party backend.

2. Direct Network Communication

Every API request made by MySLT Tracker goes directly over HTTPS from your browser to official Sri Lanka Telecom (SLT) endpoints under https://omniscapp.slt.lk/slt/ext/api/*. No proxy server or intermediary is used.

3. Permissions & Host Access Justification

Permission / Host Why It Is Required
storage Stores your token, telephone number, account number, and notice dismissal flag locally in chrome.storage.local.
scripting Injects the portal sync & banner scripts into already-open https://myslt.slt.lk/* tabs when installed or started.
declarativeNetRequestWithHostAccess Applies static header rules (rules.json) to set the required Origin and Referer headers on direct API calls to https://omniscapp.slt.lk/slt/ext/api/.
https://myslt.slt.lk/* Reads active MySLT portal session storage (slt_accessToken, selectedAcc) when you visit the portal and renders the optional top summary banner.
https://omniscapp.slt.lk/* Allows direct read-only requests to SLT's UsageSummary, ExtraGB, VAS Bundles, and BillPaymentRequest APIs.

4. How We Use Information & Limited Use Compliance

Information accessed by the extension is used exclusively inside your browser to display your SLT broadband usage and billing information. We never sell, rent, transfer, monetize, or use user data for advertising or tracking.

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Deleting Your Data

You remain in full control of your stored data at all times:

  • Open the extension settings (⚙) and click Reset Settings to immediately erase sltToken, sltPhone, and sltAcc from chrome.storage.local.
  • Uninstalling the extension from Chrome permanently deletes all locally stored extension data.
