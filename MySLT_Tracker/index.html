Monitor Your SLT Broadband Usage & Bill Directly in Chrome

MySLT Tracker is an open-source, unofficial companion extension that displays your SLT data packages, remaining quota breakdown, and monthly billing status in a fast toolbar popup or full-screen dashboard — with zero data collection and no external servers.

Notice & Feedback — Tested on SLT Fibre Only: This extension is currently tested on SLT Fibre connections only. If it works on ADSL or 4G, or if you find any bugs or have feedback, please send a message!

Single Purpose Statement

MySLT Tracker has one purpose: helping signed-in Sri Lanka Telecom (SLT) broadband subscribers view their own MySLT data usage buckets and billing details locally inside the browser without manually reopening the portal each time.

Key Features

Auto-Detects Packages

Dynamically reads your main package, Free Data (Mid Night Thriller), Bonus, Extra GB, and VAS add-ons.

Unified Totals & Remaining

Displays Total Used, Total Limit (+ Unl. when applicable), and labels which packages hold your remaining GB.

Billing & Countdown

Shows current balance/credit, last bill, due date, last payment, and Next Bill cycle countdown (YYYY/MM/01).

Popup, Dashboard & Portal Bar

Compact popup, responsive full-screen view, and an optional top bar with Copy Token on myslt.slt.lk.
